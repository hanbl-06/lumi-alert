Why we sell this way

A sensor is a product. A maintained lighting network is a relationship.

The companies that already keep lights on — electrical contractors, lighting supply houses, controls integrators and facilities-services firms — have something Lumi Alert cannot build quickly or cheaply: trust with the people who own the fixtures, crews certified to work at height on energized circuits, and a service agreement that renews every year.

Trying to compete with that would be slow and would leave customers worse off, because the company that installs a sensor should be the company that answers when a fault code appears at 11pm. So we build the hardware, the mesh, the firmware and the dashboard, and we hand the customer relationship to the partner who earned it.

Our partners don't resell a box. They sell the end of guesswork — and then they're the ones who get paid to fix what the box finds.

That matters more than the hardware margin. When a partner installs Lumi Alert across 4,000 fixtures, they stop bidding against the cheapest relamping quote in town and start holding a data stream that tells them, every single night, exactly which fixtures need work. The failures that used to surface as angry phone calls become scheduled, routed, pre-diagnosed work orders. That is a better business to be in, and it is defensible in a way that per-fixture labor pricing never is.

    Straight answers

    What partners ask before they sign.

    Will Lumi Alert sell directly to my customer?

    No. Registered accounts are yours for the life of the deployment, and inbound leads from your territory are routed to you. We quote hardware and subscription to partners, not to end customers — the only exception is a pilot a partner declines to take, and we tell you before that happens.

    Do I have to carry inventory?

    Only distributors do. Certified Installers order per project with modules shipping in 10 business days, and we hold buffer stock for registered deals so a go-ahead doesn't stall on hardware.

    What happens to my service revenue?

    It's yours, in full, and it tends to grow. Lumi Alert converts unpredictable complaint-driven callouts into scheduled, pre-diagnosed work orders, which is easier to staff and easier to price. Partners generally bill the same or more labor while spending far less time diagnosing on a ladder.

    Is the territory exclusive?

    Founding-cohort partners get first right of refusal in their metro for 24 months, plus locked pricing. It is not blanket exclusivity — if a partner stops deploying, the territory reopens. We'd rather be honest about that up front than write it in small print.

    What if the sensor fails in the field?

    The mesh reports its own dead modules, so you learn about it before the customer does. Five-year warranty, advance replacement, and warranty swaps don't come out of your labor — we credit the visit on registered deployments.

    How early-stage is this, really?

    Early. Lumi Alert is a student-founded venture building its first partner cohort, and the figures on this site are modeled rather than audited. The upside of talking to us now is pricing and territory terms that won't exist once the network fills in; the risk is that you're betting on a young company. Both of those are true, and you should weigh them.
